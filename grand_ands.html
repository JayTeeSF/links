Grand "Ands" of Scripture - Bible Verses to Help
Grand And(s) Passage Reference Description
Grace AND Peace "Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ." Romans 1 7 "A common greeting in Paul's epistles, encapsulating Christian doctrine."
Faith AND Works "Faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead." James 2 17 "Emphasizes the inseparable nature of true faith and righteous action."
The Spirit AND The Bride "The Spirit and the bride say, 'Come!'" Revelation 22 17 "Symbolizes the unified call of the Holy Spirit and the Church for Christ's return."
Signs AND Wonders "And by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders wrought among the people." Acts 5 12 "Highlights the miraculous as both a sign of divine approval and a wonder eliciting awe."
Justice AND Righteousness "But let justice roll down as waters, and righteousness as an ever-flowing stream." Amos 5 24 "Depicts justice and righteousness as inseparable elements of God's character."
Goodness AND Mercy "Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life." Psalm 23 6 "Illustrates the continual presence of God's kindness and compassion in one's life."
Life AND Peace "For to set the mind on the flesh is death, but to set the mind on the Spirit is life and peace." Romans 8 6 "Contrasts the outcomes of spiritual versus fleshly focus."
Law AND Prophets "Do not think that I have come to abolish the Law or the Prophets; I have not come to abolish them but to fulfill them." Matthew 5 17 "Jesus' affirmation of His role in fulfilling Old Testament scripture."
Love AND Truth "Love does not delight in evil but rejoices with the truth." 1 Corinthians 13 6 "Highlights the alignment of love with truth rather than with wrongdoing."
Light AND Darkness "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." John 1 5 "Symbolizes the victory of divine light over darkness."
Strength AND Song "The LORD is my strength and my song; he has become my salvation." Exodus 15 2 "Expresses reliance on God for power and as a source of joy."
Faith AND Patience "So that you may not be sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises." Hebrews 6 12 "Links faith with patience in the pursuit of God's promises."
Mercy AND Truth "Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other." Psalm 85 10 "Portrays the harmonious meeting of mercy with truth, and righteousness with peace."
Spirit AND Truth "God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth." John 4 24 "Emphasizes the need for worship that is both spiritually sincere and aligned with truth."
Heaven AND Earth "Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away." Matthew 24 35 "Affirms the enduring nature of Jesus' teachings despite the temporal nature of the physical world."
Water AND Spirit "Jesus answered, 'Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.'" John 3 5 "Underlines the necessity of spiritual rebirth for entry into the divine kingdom."
Sorrow AND Joy "Your sorrow will turn into joy." John 16 20 "Promises the transformation of suffering into joy, illustrating the redemptive potential of divine intervention."
Wealth AND Riches "Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever." Psalm 112 3 "Links material blessing with enduring righteousness as signs of divine favor."
Honor AND Glory "To him be glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen." 1 Peter 5 11 "Ascribes eternal glory and authority to God,reflecting His supreme sovereignty."
Prayer AND Supplication "Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God." Philippians 4 6 "Advises the use of prayer and supplication as means to address anxiety and present needs to God."
Word AND Spirit "For the word of God is living and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword, it penetrates even to dividing soul and spirit, joints and marrow; it judges the thoughts and attitudes of the heart." Hebrews 4 12 "Highlights the power of God's Word to discern truth and innermost thoughts, coupled with the Spirit's work."
Hope AND Salvation "For God did not appoint us to suffer wrath but to receive salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ. He died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with him. Therefore encourage one another and build each other up, just as in fact you are doing." 1 Thessalonians 5 9-11 "Emphasizes the assurance of salvation and hope in Christ, encouraging mutual support among believers."
Grace, Mercy, AND Peace "Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord." 1 Timothy 1 2 "A triad of divine blessings offered to believers, encompassing grace, mercy, and peace as foundational elements of the Christian life."
Faith, Hope, AND Love "And now these three remain: faith, hope and love. But the greatest of these is love." 1 Corinthians 13 13 "The trio of cardinal Christian virtues, with love highlighted as the supreme quality that outlasts all others."
Gold, Silver, AND Precious Stones "Now if any man build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble;" 1 Corinthians 3 12 "Metaphors for the varying quality and enduring nature of the work one does in life, especially in the context of Christian ministry."
Prophets, Apostles, AND Jesus Christ "having been built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the chief cornerstone," Ephesians 2 20 "Describes the foundation of the Christian church as being built on the teachings of the apostles and prophets, with Jesus as the pivotal cornerstone."
Kingdom, Power, AND Glory "For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen." Matthew 6 13 "A doxology affirming God's sovereignty, omnipotence, and majesty, commonly recited in the Lord's Prayer."
Repentance, Baptism, AND Forgiveness of Sins "Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit." Acts 2 38 "Links repentance and baptism as prerequisites for forgiveness and the receipt of the Holy Spirit."
Past, Present, AND Future "Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever." Hebrews 13 8 "Affirms the unchanging nature of Christ across all time, providing a foundation for faith and hope."
Body, Soul, AND Spirit "May your whole spirit, soul and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ." 1 Thessalonians 5 23 "A prayer for the holistic sanctification of the believer, touching on the comprehensive aspects of human nature."
