Why This Page?

I created this page to share my favorite "media" with family and friends. Whether you're in a show-hole or looking for a great new movie, TV series, podcast, or other content, this page will help you discover something worthwhile.

My star ratings are as follows:

  • 1-2 stars: Meh. Skip it unless you're really interested in the topic.
  • 3 stars: Entertaining. Worth a watch/listen if you like the genre.
  • 4 stars: Great content. Highly recommended.
  • 5 stars: Must-watch! Even if you're not a fan of the genre, it's too good to miss.

How to Use This Page

  • Search: Use the search bar to find content by keyword, title, or tags (e.g., "action," "comedy").
  • Filter: Use the "Select Tags" dropdown to narrow results. You can combine multiple tags for advanced filtering.
  • Sort: Use the "Sort By" dropdown to organize results by title or rating (ascending or descending).
  • Tabs:
    • Favorites: By default, the page shows my favorite links.
    • All Links: Switch to this tab to view everything without any favorites filter.
  • Clear Filters: Removes all applied filters, such as search terms, selected tags, and sorting preferences, to show an unrestricted view of the current tab (Favorites or All Links).
  • Modal View: Click a thumbnail to view a larger version of the image in a pop-up modal.
